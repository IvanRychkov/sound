About

Hi there!

I'm Ivan Rychkov, a live sound engineer.

I've been in the profession since 2016Since 2011 if you count college... since 2009 if you count demos on a dictaphone... since the last century if you count everything.. I've worked everywhere — from open-air stages and cozy clubs to jams, weddings, corporate events, so nothing surprises me anymore.

Club concerts are closest to my heart. Five years at the Alexey Kozlov Club were my main school (FOH + MON).

These days I prefer project-based work where I can prepare properly and give it my all.

Based in Belgrade (long story). I speak English fluently and can communicate with local crew.

If you're an artist (or you organise concerts) and care about great sound and easy communication — I'd love to take your show!
